# FAUCHAR visual guidelines — Instagram launch

## Mark

The logo is a lower molar built from 56 voxels on an 8 × 10 grid. Fifty-five cells are ivory. One cell, on the occlusal surface, is signal lime. That cell is the model's signal. Do not move it, recolor the whole mark, add a glow, or redraw the tooth.

On graphite (#0B1114) the cells are #F5F4EF and the signal is #B7F34A.
On ivory the cells are #0B1114 and the signal is #6F9A1F, so it stays readable.

## Color

- Graphite #0B1114 — background
- Signal lime #B7F34A — one cell, one emphasized word, labels
- Ivory #F5F4EF — headlines and the mark
- Slate #8A929A — footer, numbers, secondary labels
- Mist #D9DCD6 — light surfaces only

Lime is never a background fill on the feed, except a single word or a one-pixel-weight arc.

Feed posts sit on a faint ivory voxel field, denser toward the edges, with a dark veil under the headline so the type stays readable. A few lime cells appear only at the outer edge. Stories stay on flat graphite.

## Type

Headlines: Space Grotesk Medium, tight tracking.
Text and labels: Inter.
The wordmark is FAUCHAR in Space Grotesk, tracked, with DENTAL INTELLIGENCE underneath.

## Frames

Feed posts are 1080 × 1350. Instagram's profile grid shows the center 1080 × 1080, so the logo and the headline sit inside that square. The lockup and the footer sit just inside the crop, not in the outer 135 px.

Stories are 1080 × 1920. Type stays clear of the top 280 px and the bottom 280 px, where Instagram places the profile and the reply bar.

Margins on the feed are 72 px from the side edges.

## Voice on the artwork

Say that FAUCHAR is building AI-assisted crown and bridge design, and that the professional reviews the result.
Do not put accuracy percentages, clinical-validation claims, or "available now" on a frame.
