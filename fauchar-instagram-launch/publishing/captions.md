## 03 — From scan to restoration.

```
The case does not end at the scan.

The workflow we are building runs from a 3D dental scan to a restoration a professional can still edit. Read the anatomy. Propose the crown or bridge. Review the margin, the contacts, and the occlusion. Export an STL for the laboratory.

This is the sequence. It is not a claim that every step is finished.
```
