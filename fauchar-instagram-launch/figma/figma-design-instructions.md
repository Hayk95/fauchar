## What is editable

Each feed post is its own 1080 × 1350 frame. Each story is its own 1080 × 1920 frame. Headlines, body text, labels, and the footer are text. The molar is the real voxel logo, drawn as vector squares, not a generated picture. Colors are the brand hex values.

To change a headline, open the feed or stories page and edit the text in that frame. To export, select one frame and export PNG at 1x. The feed exports at 1080 × 1350. Stories export at 1080 × 1920.

The PNG files already exported for publishing are in `images/`, `stories/`, and `previews/`. Copies also sit in `figma/figma-import-assets/`.

## If a page looks empty

Refresh the Figma file. Captures land as separate pages. The default blank page can be deleted.

## Fonts

Headlines use Space Grotesk. Text uses Inter. If Figma substitutes a font, install both on the machine that opens the file, or replace the missing font with the same family. Do not restyle the frames with a different typeface.

## What not to change

Do not redraw the molar, move the lime cell, or fill the background with lime. Do not add performance numbers to a frame.
